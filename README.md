# Hi there, I'm Cheick Himran El Fadel Moundi 👋

### Bioinformatician | PhD Researcher | Genomics & Statistical Genetics

I am a bioinformatician and doctoral researcher at the **Institut de Recherche en Sciences de la Santé (IRSS), Burkina Faso**.

I hold an MSc in Bioinformatics from the **African Center of Excellence in Bioinformatics (ACE-B), University of Sciences, Techniques and Technologies of Bamako (USTTB), Mali**, and a Bachelor's degree in Medical Biology.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **bioinformatics, genomics, human genetics, and molecular biosurveillance**, with a particular interest in research relevant to African populations.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- Polygenic Risk Scores (PRS)

- Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)
- Metagenomics and Molecular Biosurveillance
- Reproducible Bioinformatics Workflows
